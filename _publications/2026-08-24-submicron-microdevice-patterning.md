This work introduces nail-polish microfibers as accessible shadow masks for fabricating microdevices. Oxygen-plasma thinning enables submicron electrode gaps without photolithography. The method is demonstrated with MoS₂ field-effect transistors and photodetectors on rigid and flexible substrates.
